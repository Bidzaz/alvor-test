Delete your Alvor account

You can delete your account here or in the app, under Settings → Delete my account. Both do the same.

What gets deleted

  • your plants, notes, watering and activity history
  • all your photos and your profile photo
  • your profile, friends, friend requests and friend link
  • reactions and comments you wrote, and the ones on your garden
  • your notification settings and your sign-in

Everything goes straight away, from every phone, and can't be undone. Your friends aren't told; you simply disappear from their list. Want a copy first? In the app, tap Settings → Download my data.

Confirm it's you

Forgot your password? Open Alvor, tap "Forgot your password?" to set a new one, then come back. Or write to from your account's email.

Your account has been deleted

Everything in it is gone. If Alvor is installed on a phone, you can uninstall it. Thank you for growing with Alvor.
