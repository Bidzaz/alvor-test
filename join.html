Alvor

Get Alvor

The app for your plants: frost and heat warnings, watering reminders, plant identification, and a small circle of friends who see each other's gardens.

1Install Alvor

Alvor isn't in the Play Store yet, so it comes as a file you install yourself.

Download Alvor for Android

Open the file when it has downloaded. Android will warn you about apps from outside the Play Store and ask to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install.

Already have Alvor? Skip to step 2.

2Open Alvor

Open Alvor and tap Create account. You'll need the invite code you were sent.

Open Alvor with my invitation

If Alvor opens without your invitation, and paste it into "Invite code or link".

Use Alvor in your browser

The Alvor app is for Android for now. On this device you can use Alvor in the browser, with the same account.

Open Alvor

Tip: in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen to keep Alvor like an app.

Or use Alvor in the browser instead.
